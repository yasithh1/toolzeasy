10 Uses for QR Codes in 2026

Useful QR ideas for shops, classrooms, creators, local services and events.

QR codes are still useful because they remove typing. One scan can open a menu, form, map, payment page, portfolio, sign-up page or support link.

10 practical QR code ideas

  1. Restaurant and cafe menus.
  2. Google Forms for event registration.
  3. Business cards that open a portfolio or LinkedIn page.
  4. Product packaging with instructions or warranty details.
  5. Classroom worksheets that open extra resources.
  6. Real estate signs with property pages.
  7. Wi-Fi instructions for guests.
  8. Posters that link to ticket pages.
  9. Creator pages for YouTube, TikTok or newsletters.
  10. Customer feedback forms after a service.
