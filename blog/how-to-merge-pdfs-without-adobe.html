How to Merge PDFs Without Adobe

Combine PDF files online for free with no login, no signup and no paid PDF editor.

If you only need to combine a few documents, you do not need a full desktop PDF editor. A browser-based PDF merger can join reports, forms, scanned pages, invoices and school documents in a few clicks.

Best way to merge PDFs online

  1. Open the free PDF Merge tool.
  2. Click Add PDF files and choose two or more documents.
  3. Remove any file you do not want in the final PDF.
  4. Click PDF Merge and download the combined file.

Why use a no-login PDF merger?

No-login tools are faster for simple jobs. You do not need to remember a password, verify an email address or install extra software. ToolzEasy is designed for quick upload, process and download workflows.

Common PDF merging examples

  • Combine invoice pages before emailing a client.
  • Join scanned forms into one file for school or work.
  • Merge separate report chapters into one document.
  • Put receipts, IDs or supporting files into one PDF.
